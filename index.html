Oboro 是設計工作室 LiminalSynths延伸出的燈具創作系列，作品以 3D 列印與手工少量製作。

Oboro is a lighting collection that grew out of the design studio LiminalSynths. Each piece is made in small batches through 3D printing and handcraft.

Dimensions: W 9.7 × H 16 cm

Light source: LED panel

Material: PETG

Color temperature: 3 modes

Power: USB Type-A, DC 5V

Wattage: 3W

Recommended: USB adapter 5V/1A or above (not included)
